## FAQ Copy (from landing page — use in email FAQ section)

**What is a prediction market?**
A market that lets you trade on the outcome of a real-world event. The price of a "Yes" share reflects the market's collective probability — if it trades at 67¢, the crowd believes there's roughly a 67% chance. Hold to settlement and a winning share pays out at full value.

**Is this the same as gambling?**
No. Prices are set by traders weighing real information, not by a house edge. You can buy and sell your position at any time before an event resolves, and markets settle transparently on-chain.

**Do I need to move funds out of Zengo?**
Never. Your positions and settlements live inside your self-custodial Zengo wallet. No bridges, no separate exchange accounts — your keys, secured by MPC, stay with you.

**How is Zengo different from MetaMask?**
Zengo replaces the vulnerable seed phrase with MPC. Your key is split so there's no single point of failure, and 3-Factor recovery means you can always get back in — even if you lose your phone.

**When do I get my winnings?**
As soon as a market resolves, winning contracts settle automatically in USDC to your prediction account. Withdraw to your Zengo wallet balance at any time.

- Never "prediction market" — use "Zengo markets", "trade on outcomes", "markets"
- Orange = energy, conviction, winning. Match copy intensity to the color.
- Settlement language: "settles in USDC", "straight into your wallet", "no bridges, no volatility."
